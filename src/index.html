⚠ Mismatch: the feedback line doesn't match the commanded state. The relay may be stuck, the driver blown, or the sense wire broken.
Calibration

The feedback line is read on the ADC through a 10K resistor. Anything at or above the threshold counts as "pump energised". The output drives 3.3 V when enabled, so the sense line pegs high — a midpoint threshold (~1600 mV) cleanly separates on from off.

LED behavior

Errors only (default): off when everything's fine, brief blink for WiFi faults, triple-blink for a pump feedback mismatch. Off: never lights up. Debug: solid on when WiFi connected.

Upload a compiled firmware image (.bin — built with pio run, found under .pio/build/<env>/firmware.bin). The device writes it to the inactive slot, then reboots into the new image. The pump boots OFF after the update. Don't power off during the upload.
